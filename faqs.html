Returns & Exchanges

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery on unworn, unwashed items with original tags. Some items like final sale or underwear are non-returnable.

Just head to our Returns Portal, enter your order number and email, and follow the steps. You’ll receive a prepaid return label if eligible.

Once we receive and inspect your return, refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to your original payment method.

Absolutely! If the size you need is available, we’ll process an exchange once your original item is received.

Ordering & Payment

We accept Visa, Mastercard, AMEX, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Velora gift cards.

Shipping & Delivery

We currently ship across the U.S. and select international countries. Shipping options and costs are calculated at checkout.

Standard shipping takes 3–7 business days. Expedited options are available at checkout.

If your tracking hasn’t updated in 5+ days or your package is missing, reach out to our Support Team and we’ll help resolve it ASAP.
